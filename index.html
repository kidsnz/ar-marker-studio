6. Where the artwork is weak

How to get more points (everything below is measured, not guessed)
  • Scale up 2× with nearest-neighbour. By far the most effective. Interpolated scaling backfires.
  • Use -level=4. Going from the default 2 almost doubles the count.
  • Make the artwork coarser. Place large blocks apart from each other.
  • Put structure on all four edges. Top and bottom alone cannot fix horizontal position.
  • Go square or 4:3. Wide formats lose badly when the phone is held upright.
  • Keep the greys. Converting to pure black and white makes it far worse.

Confirmed not to help: thin lines, dithering, double or dashed lines, thin borders (under 3px after downscaling), changing -dpi (it only shifts the distance bands), and 4× scaling (worse than 2×).
